About Me

Hi, I'm a Biochemistry graduate, a Full-Stack Developer in training, and an entrepreneur passionate about technology, innovation, and building solutions that make everyday life better.

My interest in technology and problem-solving has led me into software development, where I am developing my skills in building functional, user-focused digital experiences and turning ideas into practical solutions.

Building Dhub has given me firsthand experience in entrepreneurship, business development, customer relations, and the importance of creating something people can trust.

My journey brings together science, technology, and business, three fields that continue to shape how I think, learn, and approach challenges. I believe in continuous learning, intentional growth, and the power of combining different perspectives to create meaningful impact.

I am constantly exploring new technologies, improving my development skills, and looking for opportunities to collaborate, build innovative products, and grow alongside people who are passionate about solving real-world problems.

I am still learning, still building, and becoming better with every step. My goal is to create technology-driven solutions, build sustainable businesses, and make a meaningful contribution wherever my skills and ideas can make a difference.
